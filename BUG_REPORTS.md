# Reporting a bug

Project World Music writes its own log, so a bug report is just a file away.

**The log:** `Download/ProjectWorld/log.txt` (this session) and `log_prev.txt` (the one before).

1. Make the problem happen.
2. Close the game. **Don't open it again yet**: opening it moves the log to `log_prev.txt`.
3. Open an [issue](../../issues) with what you did, what happened, and `log.txt` attached. A screenshot of it is fine too.

**Crashed?** The next start shows the crash right away. Screenshot it, or send `log_prev.txt`.

**Chart Import problem?** Also send `Download/ProjectWorld/converter/status.txt` and what Termux printed.
